Privacy Policy — Warehouse Inventory

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

Warehouse Inventory (package name: com.justtony.warehouse) is an Android application developed by JustTony for managing warehouse stock, materials, storage boxes, supply orders, customer orders, and QR-coded storage operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App or contact the developer. Privacy contact: antonryzhkov199876@gmail.com.

2. Information Handled by the App

The App does not require account creation, login, or the submission of names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.

You may enter or create warehouse information, including stock-keeping units (SKUs), product quantities, box and storage-section identifiers, QR-code values, and supply or customer order numbers. According to the App’s intended design, this operational information does not include customers’ personal details.

Warehouse information is stored locally on your Android device. The developer does not operate a server for storing or synchronizing this information and does not receive it through the App.

The App does not send warehouse records, stored QR-code values, or locally generated diagnostic reports to the developer or to a warehouse-data server. Google ML Kit, which is used for QR-code scanning, separately sends certain technical metrics to Google as described below.

3. Camera and QR-Code Scanning

The App may request access to your device camera to scan QR codes used in warehouse operations. QR-code image processing and recognition are performed on the device using Google ML Kit. According to Google, ML Kit does not send input images or resulting QR-code contents to Google servers. Camera images and QR-code contents are not sent to the developer.

You can manage camera permission in your device settings. Denying permission will prevent features that require scanning from using the camera.

4. Google ML Kit and Technical Metrics

The App uses Google ML Kit for on-device QR-code scanning. ML Kit automatically sends technical information to Google, which may include device and operating-system information, application name and version, installation-related identifiers, SDK performance metrics, configuration, SDK usage events, and error codes. Depending on the scanning features enabled, ML Kit may also collect technical information about auto-zoom events.

Google uses these metrics for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, improvements, and detection of misuse or abuse. According to Google, these metrics are encrypted in transit using HTTPS and are not shared by ML Kit with third parties.

For additional details, see Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy and ML Kit Android Data Disclosure. Google processes the technical data under its applicable privacy terms.

The App does not contain advertisements or developer-operated behavioral analytics. The developer does not sell warehouse information or personal data.

Any diagnostic report generated by the App is saved locally on the device and is not automatically transmitted to the developer or any third party.

5. Local Storage, Security, and Backups

The App retains warehouse information on your device for as long as you keep it there. Android provides device-level and application-level security controls; you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

The developer cannot remotely view, recover, or delete your local warehouse information.

Your Android device may provide operating-system backup, restore, or device-transfer functions. Depending on device settings and application backup configuration, those features may create copies outside the App. The developer does not operate or access such backups.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Warehouse records remain on your device until you remove them using available App functions, clear the App’s storage through Android settings, or uninstall the App. Clearing App storage or uninstalling generally removes the App’s private local data from that device. Copies in device backups or files separately saved by you may remain and must be managed separately.

Because the App has no user accounts and the developer does not receive warehouse records, there is no remote account or remote warehouse-data record for the developer to delete.

Email communications are processed using our email service provider and may be handled according to that provider’s privacy and security practices. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry and related follow-up, or longer where required by applicable law, and then delete it when no longer needed.

You may contact us at antonryzhkov199876@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held in our correspondence, subject to applicable law. Where applicable, requests will be handled under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” and other relevant data-protection laws.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for warehouse-management activities and is not directed to children. The App does not offer user accounts and does not intentionally request personal information from children.
