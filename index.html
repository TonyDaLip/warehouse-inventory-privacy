Privacy Policy — Warehouse Inventory

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

Warehouse Inventory (package name: com.justtony.warehouse) is an Android application developed by JustTony for managing warehouse stock, materials, storage boxes, supply orders, customer orders, and QR-coded storage operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App or contact the developer. Privacy contact: antonryzhkov199876@gmail.com.

2. Warehouse Data and Settings

No account or login is required. Warehouse operations work locally on your device; the developer does not operate a server for storing or synchronizing your warehouse.

Information you enter or import can include SKUs, descriptions, quantities, box and section identifiers, barcode and QR-code values, orders, supplier or recipient information, comments, and import metadata. The App also stores warehouse operation history and settings such as your selected language. Names, contact details, or other personal information may be present if you include them in these records or imported files.

These records support the warehouse functions you use. They are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Technical processing by Google ML Kit is described separately below.

The App contains no advertisements and does not automatically upload usage analytics to the developer. The developer does not sell your personal information or warehouse data.

3. Camera, Barcodes, and QR Codes

The App requests camera access for barcode and QR-code scanning. Recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device. According to Google's ML Kit privacy documentation, input images and recognition results are processed locally and are not sent to Google servers. The App does not upload camera images or scanned code contents to the developer.

You can grant or revoke camera permission in Android settings. Without it, camera scanning is unavailable. The App does not record audio for scanning.

4. Google ML Kit Technical Data

Google documents that ML Kit SDKs collect and send technical metrics to Google, separately from the images and code contents being recognized. These metrics can include device and operating-system information, app package and version, installation-related identifiers, performance measurements, SDK configuration, usage events, and error codes.

Google describes using these metrics for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, improvements, and abuse prevention. According to Google, the disclosed metrics are encrypted in transit using HTTPS and are not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. See the ML Kit Android data disclosure, ML Kit Terms and Privacy, and Google Privacy Policy.

This SDK processing is separate from the App's local diagnostic journal and saved reports described below. Saving or deleting an App report does not control Google's SDK metrics.

5. Local Diagnostics and Saved Reports

The App keeps a limited diagnostic journal on the device to help investigate faults. It records selected operation stages, timestamps, results, technical error codes, and sanitized diagnostic details. It is not a complete recording of everything you do.

A diagnostic report you create can contain:

  • App and build versions, database schema version, and selected language.
  • Device model and Android version, and an indicator of a loaded test dataset.
  • Journal events, error details, and temporary identifiers used to relate events within a run or report.
  • Any problem description or other text you choose to enter in the report form.

The report does not automatically attach your warehouse database, warehouse record contents, original import documents, camera images, or scanned code contents. Technical fields do not include a phone serial number or advertising identifier. Text you enter yourself is included as entered and may contain personal or warehouse information.

You can save the report as a text file using the system folder picker. The App has no function for sending the report to the developer and does not upload it automatically. You can separately choose to send the saved file using another application. If you send it to support, section 8 applies.

6. Files, Backups, and Security

You choose files to import or restore, and destinations for saved reports, backups, and history archives. Imports retain structured records and import metadata; original spreadsheet documents are not kept as a permanent document library in the App. Temporary working copies are managed by the App.

The App can generate PNG label images and save them to the device's gallery. Depending on Android version, it may request image or storage permission for this operation. The label-saving feature does not upload your gallery to the developer.

Exported backups contain warehouse data and can replace the current warehouse when you confirm restoration. History archives contain exported records for use outside the App. Backups, archives, saved diagnostic reports, and label images are not encrypted by the App. Protect them and share them only with intended recipients.

If the system picker offers a cloud storage provider and you select it, that provider may store the chosen file outside your phone under its own privacy and security terms. The developer does not receive those files merely because you save them there.

Private App storage is protected by Android's application isolation and your device security settings. The App also keeps limited internal recovery copies for safe restoration. Standard Android automatic backup is disabled in the App configuration; manufacturer-specific device-transfer behavior may vary. The developer cannot remotely inspect, restore, or erase your local warehouse.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Current warehouse records and information required for active work remain on your device. Archiving an item or cancelling an order does not erase its confirmed history. Eligible completed history can be removed through a separately confirmed cleanup after a verified history export. Cleanup protects the most recent 90 days of operational history and information still required by active records; it does not automatically erase every record older than 90 days.

The operational diagnostic journal uses a seven-day retention window and a 10 MiB size limit. Cleanup runs while the App operates, so expiry is not a guarantee of deletion at an exact time while the App is closed. Temporary generated reports use a one-day cleanup threshold and a two-report limit, with protection for files currently in use. These limits do not remove copies you saved outside private App storage.

The App rotates internal recovery copies and cleans obsolete working files. Copies or recovery information needed for an interrupted restoration may be retained until it is safe to remove them. Restoring a warehouse backup does not reset all installation settings or erase the separate diagnostic journal.

Clearing App storage in Android settings or uninstalling generally removes private local App data. Exported files, gallery images, cloud copies, and files sent to others may remain; manage those copies separately. There is no remote App account or developer-hosted warehouse to delete.

8. Support Correspondence and Your Requests

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, Telegram, or another communication service, we receive the contact or profile information that service makes available, your message, and any files you choose to attach. This may include a saved diagnostic report. Sending an attachment is your choice; the App does not send it on your behalf.

We use the received information to investigate the reported issue, respond, and handle related follow-up. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it. Email and messaging providers process communications under their own privacy and security terms. We retain correspondence and attachments only as long as reasonably necessary for the inquiry and related follow-up, or longer where required by applicable law, and delete them when no longer needed.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held in support correspondence by writing to antonryzhkov199876@gmail.com. Requests are handled in accordence with applicable law. This does not give the developer access to data that remains only on your device or control over copies held independently by you or other recipients.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for warehouse-management activities and is not directed to children. It does not offer user accounts or intentionally request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer through support, please contact us so we can review the request and remove that information where appropriate.
